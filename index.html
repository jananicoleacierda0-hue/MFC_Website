HOW TO ORDER

Gutom ka na ba? Eto na, Bes!

This site is a menu, not a checkout — browse lang, then order directly through Messenger.

01

Browse our Menu

Tingin-tingin muna sa menu namin at piliin ang cravings mo.

Menu namin dito →

⚠️ Warning: Nakakagutom mag-browse!

NEXTSTEP 1.5

Copy the Blank Order Form

Paste it in Messenger, then fill in your details and orders.

02

Paste & Fill Up the Form

  • Name & contact
  • Address & pin location
  • Payment method
  • Delivery date & time
  • Orders (brand/flavor/qty)

Send your completed form through Messenger / Instagram.

03

Wait for Confirmation

We'll check availability, confirm if it's same-day or pre-order, and compute your total.

No #HopiaMoments! 😉

04

Pay First Policy

Once confirmed, Pay First tayo. No COD because most items are made-to-order.

Send your payment screenshot to confirm your reservation.

05

Waiting Time

For same-day orders, preparation may take around 1–2 hours, depending on your items.

Tip: Pre-order for faster and smoother service!

06

Delivery Time!

We can book through Lalamove, or you can book your own courier after the “Food is Ready” signal.

Delivery fees are checked on the day of delivery.

ℹ️

No cart, no online payment, no delivery tracking here. Browse the menu, then confirm your order directly through Messenger.
